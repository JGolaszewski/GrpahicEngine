# Shadow caster behavior

Opaque materials cast solid shadows when their entity has `CastShadows` enabled. Masked materials sample base-color alpha and use the material alpha cutoff, so cutouts match forward rendering. Blended materials do not cast shadows; they represent partial coverage that this depth-only shadow map cannot model. Shadow culling follows the material's double-sided setting.

The scene light component supports **Directional**, **Point**, and **Spot** types. Directional lights use the entity's rotated emission direction and do not use a range or cone. Their orthographic shadow view fits the active camera frusta up to 120 world units, snaps its center to shadow texels, and reserves bounded upstream depth for casters. Off-camera geometry therefore cannot expand receiver coverage and lower near-camera detail. The Settings panel's **Directional shadow fit diagnostics** readout reports active coverage, map resolution, world-space texel size, and the light's authored depth bias. Point lights illuminate from the entity position within their configured range and use six shadow views to cover every direction around the light. Spot lights use the entity position, emission direction, range, and inner/outer cone angles. All three light types can cast shadows when enabled, subject to the configured shadow caster limit. The Inspector only shows controls that apply to the selected type. Mesh material overrides are authored inline on the mesh entity and are saved with the scene.
